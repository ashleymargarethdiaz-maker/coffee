    We roast what we serve.

    Sundial started with a second-hand roaster and a single question: what does this coffee taste like an hour earlier or later in the roast?

    Today we buy directly from three farms, roast twice a week, and sell every bag within ten days of roasting. If you want to taste the difference, order the same bean as espresso and as filter.
